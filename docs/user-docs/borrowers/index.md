# Overview

Pipeline finances physical commodity trade through onboarded Originators. Traders who complete onboarding (KYB, AML and sanctions checks) can borrow from the protocol for trade flows that fit Pipeline's mandate: eligible commodities, tenors of up to 180 days, physical collateral and a contracted sale. Originators handle structuring, documentation and monitoring.

## How to access Pipeline

Capital flows to borrowers through Originators. The Originator underwrites, structures and monitors your facility, and lends in it alongside a bankruptcy-remote lending vehicle that lends the pool's capital. Both lend on the same terms. The Risk Council approves each facility, and every facility is recorded on-chain. Recourse is to you, the borrower, in full.

See [For Originators](/originators/). If you have an active trade flow that fits eligibility, contact an Originator directly.

Tenors run up to 180 days. For an open-account sale, the advance ratio is at least 10 points lower and the buyer needs credit insurance.

## Facility size

$500K to $20M per facility. Larger facilities are considered case by case.

## Documentation

Documentation runs through the Originator. Each facility is made under a master facility agreement (MFA) and an agency deed, with security documents per jurisdiction. Standard terms cover the security package (bills of lading, warehouse pledges, collateral management agreements, assignment of receivables), independent inspections, insurance, sanctions, force majeure, default triggers and enforcement.

An independent Security Agent holds the security on trust for all lenders and records each instrument in the on-chain security registry. Bills of lading are endorsed to it by way of pledge. The lenders never take title to the goods. Pipeline is named as financier in your purchase contract, by an addendum your seller acknowledges, and your buyer is notified that the sale proceeds are assigned. A local legal opinion confirms the security ranks first before funding. You appoint and pay an independent inspector from Pipeline's panel to check the goods. When the goods move, for example from a vessel into a warehouse, the Security Agent hands the security over from the old instrument to the new one.

You open a collection account at an investment-grade bank, charged to the Security Agent, which co-signs it. You pay your equity contribution into it before the drawdown. Every drawdown is paid into the account, and the Security Agent releases it to your seller only against the seller's documents of title. Receipts from your buyer pass from the account to the lending vehicle.

Each loan has an advance ratio set by commodity class. LTV is the outstanding amount plus accrued interest, divided by the value of the security. Goods count at the inspected quantity times the market price. Cash cover, receivables, letter-of-credit proceeds and prepayments count at face value. Insurance does not count. If LTV rises 5 points above the advance ratio, a margin call opens: you restore the advance ratio within 3 business days, in cash into the collection account or by partial repayment. At 10 points above, the Risk Council may declare a default.

Interest is invoiced monthly in arrears and paid separately from principal.

## What we do not finance

- Sanctioned counterparties or cargo origins and destinations
- Borrowers in Mainland PRC or other jurisdictions with capital controls, including Türkiye and Vietnam
- Commodities outside the eligible list
- Jurisdictions and corridors outside the approved list, and financing through the Red Sea, the Gulf of Aden or the Black Sea
- Tenors beyond 180 days
- Structures without first-ranking security over the goods
- Pre-shipment advances of any kind, sea waybills as title, production or transformation finance
