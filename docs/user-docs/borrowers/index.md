# Overview

Pipeline finances physical commodity trade through onboarded Originators. Traders who completed onboarding (KYB, KYT, AML check, sanctions screening) can get senior capital from the protocol for trade flows that fit Pipeline's mandate: base metals, refined oil products, softs, biofuels; 30–180 day duration; physical collateral and contracted offtake. Originators handle structuring, documentation and monitoring. Pipeline provides the senior tranche.

## How to access Pipeline

Capital flows to borrowers through Originators. The Originator underwrites, structures and monitors. It joins your facility as a lender and funds the equity tranche. A bankruptcy-remote lending vehicle is the initial lender and provides the senior capital. Pipeline provides credit approval and the public on-chain loan record. Recourse is to you, the borrower, in full.

See [For Originators](/originators/). If you have an active trade flow that fits eligibility, contact an Originator directly.

## Facility size

$500K to $20M per facility. Larger considered case by case.

## Documentation

Documentation runs through the Originator. Each facility is made under a master facility agreement (MFA) and an agency deed, with security documents per jurisdiction. Standard terms cover the security package (bills of lading, warehouse pledges, collateral management agreements, assignment of receivables), independent inspections, insurance, sanctions, force majeure, default triggers and enforcement.

An independent Security Agent holds the security on trust for all lenders and records each instrument in the on-chain security registry. Bills of lading are endorsed to it by way of pledge. The lender never takes title to the goods. Your collection account is co-signed by the Security Agent. A local legal opinion confirms the security ranks first before funding. Inspection companies appointed by the lending vehicle check the goods. When the goods move, for example from a vessel into a warehouse, the Security Agent hands the security over from the old instrument to the new one.

Each loan has an advance ratio, 80% by default, and a margin-call ratio, 85% by default. LTV is the outstanding amount plus accrued interest, divided by the value of the security. Goods count at the inspected quantity times the market price. Cash cover, receivables, letter-of-credit proceeds and prepayments count at face value. Insurance does not count. When LTV rises above the margin-call ratio, a margin call opens. You bring LTV back to the advance ratio with cash or goods within the cure period set for the loan.

## What we do not finance

- Sanctioned counterparties or cargo origins/destinations
- Commodities outside the whitelist
- Corridors outside the approved list
- Durations beyond 180 days
- Structures lacking enforceable physical-cargo security
- Uncovered advances: a supply contract plus the trader's promise, with no goods or bank undertaking behind them
