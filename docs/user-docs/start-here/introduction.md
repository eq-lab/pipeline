# Introduction to Pipeline

On-chain yield from global commodity trade.

## What Pipeline does

Pipeline is a permissioned commodity trade finance protocol. It funds short-duration loans secured by physical commodities and repaid from the sale of the goods.

Lenders deposit USDC or wire USD and receive PLUSD. 1 PLUSD is a claim on 1 USD of the pool. Lenders stake PLUSD in the staking vault and receive sPLUSD. Loan Originators source and underwrite the deals, for example, tin from Indonesia and zinc concentrate from Kazakhstan, and lend a minimum of 5–20% of each one alongside the pool. When a borrower repays, the interest net of fees is minted as PLUSD into the staking vault. The sPLUSD price rises.

Every loan is recorded on-chain. Its terms, status, disbursements, and repayments are public.

## How it differs from what exists today

|   | **Traditional trade finance fund** | **Pipeline** |
|---|---|---|
| **Access** | Quarterly subscription windows; accreditation gates | Permissioned; approved addresses deposit at any time |
| **Liquidity** | Quarterly redemptions, often gated | Instant retail withdrawals from a 10–15% liquidity buffer; larger ones at quarterly windows |
| **Transparency** | Quarterly investor letter; limited deal disclosure | Every loan on a public on-chain registry |
| **Counterparty risk** | Manager discretion on selection | Every deal is assessed against the published Credit Policy |

## How it differs from on-chain credit protocols

Existing on-chain credit pools lend USDC against borrower-pledged collateral, often with thin underwriting and weak recovery rights. They have suffered losses from undercollateralised lending and poor workouts. Pipeline differs on these points:

- A professional Loan Originator underwrites every loan and puts its own equity in the deal.
- Loans are secured by physical goods and the rights to them: bills of lading, warehouse pledges, assigned receivables. An independent Security Agent holds the security on trust for all lenders.
- Deposited USDC goes straight to a custody account. No smart contract holds lender USDC.
- The lending vehicle is bankruptcy-remote and has no beneficial owner. A licensed trustee co-signs every outflow of funds.
