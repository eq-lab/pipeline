# Overview

Earn yield from commodity trade loans. Commodity trade finance has historically had default rates below 0.3%. The loans are secured by physical goods and repaid from their sale to contracted offtakers. Each loan is capped at 80%, 75% or 65% of the value of the goods, by commodity class, and the Originator lends at least 5–20% of it on the same terms as the pool. Deposited USDC goes straight to a custody account. No smart contract holds lender USDC.

## What you get

- Yield from real commodity trade loans. Borrowers pay interest monthly
- Instant retail withdrawals from a 10–15% liquidity buffer; larger withdrawals at quarterly windows
- Every loan on a public on-chain registry

## Who can lend

Entities and individuals from non-sanctioned jurisdictions, outside the US. A USDC deposit needs a clean KYT screen of your address and the deposit. Lenders who wire USD pass KYC or KYB first.

## Minimum deposit and limits

Minimum 1,000 USDC. Per-transaction cap 5,000,000 USDC. Protocol-wide cap of 10,000,000 USDC of deposits per 24 hours, across all lenders. No protocol-level maximum beyond these rate limits.

You can also wire USD to the protocol's bank account. PLUSD is minted 1:1 for the wire and staked for you.

## How yield reaches you

Yield arrives as fresh PLUSD minted into the staking vault when loan interest is booked. Your sPLUSD share count stays the same. What each share is worth grows. There is no claim step, no restake and no compounding action.

## Risks before you deposit

Capital is exposed to credit, liquidity, custody, smart-contract, governance, regulatory and operational risks. Each one, with its mitigation and the risk left over, is in [Potential risks](/risks/). Default mechanics are in [Default management](/risks/default-management/). Read both before depositing.
