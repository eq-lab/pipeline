## 4. Advance rate and collateral cover

4.1 The Lender values the Cargo on a Net Smelter Return basis derived from the signed
offtake, the independent assay, and a gold reference price, less realisation costs, less a
40.0% haircut. The resulting figure is the **Collateral Value**.

4.2 **Collateral Coverage Ratio (CCR)** means Collateral Value divided by senior principal
outstanding, expressed in basis points.

4.3 The Borrower shall procure that CCR does not fall below the levels in clause 4.4. CCR is
monitored by the Lender and is not a condition to any drawing already made.

4.4 Monitoring levels:

| Level | CCR | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Watchlist | below 130% | Notice to Borrower via Originator. Enhanced reporting |
| Maintenance margin call | below 120% | Borrower to restore cover within 5 Business Days |
| Margin call | below 110% | Borrower to restore cover within 2 Business Days. Event of Default if unremedied |

4.5 Cover may be restored by cash prepayment, by additional concentrate delivered into the
CMA stockpile, or by cash margin into the Collateral Account.

4.6 CCR at the Utilisation Date, computed under clause 4.1 at a gold reference price of
USD 4,200.00 per troy ounce, is **147.98%**.

## 7. Application of proceeds

Every amount standing to the credit of the Collateral Account is applied in this order:

1. Senior principal outstanding.
2. Management Fee: 1.00% per annum on senior deployed, actual/365.
3. Senior coupon, net of the Management Fee and Performance Fee.
4. Performance Fee: 10% of (senior gross interest less Management Fee).
5. OET Allocation: 0.50% per annum on senior deployed, actual/365.
6. The balance to the Originator, settled outside the on-chain record.

## 8. Information undertakings

The Borrower shall deliver: weekly CMA stock reports; every inspection, weight, moisture and
assay certificate within 2 Business Days of issue; each shipping document set on
presentation; notice of any assay dispute, umpire referral, vessel deviation or AIS loss
exceeding 12 hours; and notice of any variance exceeding 3% between reported and
CMA-confirmed stock.

## 9. Events of Default (summary)

Non-payment; CCR below 110% unremedied after the cure period; breach of the CMA or removal
of Cargo without the Collateral Manager's release; insolvency; cross-default above
USD 250,000; loss of the export permit; sanctions designation of any Obligor or the Offtaker;
material misstatement in any certificate or document; repudiation of the offtake by the
Offtaker; and any failure of the security to be perfected.
