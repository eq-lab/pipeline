## 4. Chargor's representations

The Chargor is the sole legal and beneficial owner of the Goods; the Goods are free of any
other security, retention of title, lien or attachment; **no part of the Goods is the subject
of any other pledge, warehouse receipt, trust receipt or delivery order**; the offtake is in
full force and has not been assigned to anyone else; and no insolvency proceeding is pending.

## 5. Enforcement

5.1 On an Event of Default that is continuing, the Security Trustee may, without further
notice: instruct SGS to release the Goods to a liquidator; present the bills of lading and
take delivery at the discharge port; sell the Goods by private treaty or auction; collect the
Assigned Receivables directly from the Buyer; and apply the Collateral Account balance.

5.2 **Pre-onboarded liquidators.** Two concentrate liquidators are onboarded and hold current
KYB clearance so the Goods can be moved within 72 hours of a default:

| Liquidator | Capability |
|---|---|
| A Singapore-based concentrate trader | Redirects cargo afloat to an alternative Asian smelter |
| A Peruvian concentrate aggregator | Takes stockpiled material at the load port |

5.3 Enforcement in the PRC against the Buyer is by LCIA award enforced under the New York
Convention, to which the PRC is a party. Expected timeline 9 to 18 months. This is why the
structure is designed to be repaid from the controlled account rather than enforced.

## 6. Priority of proceeds

Enforcement proceeds are applied in this order:

1. Security Trustee's and liquidators' costs and expenses.
2. Senior principal outstanding.
3. Accrued senior interest and protocol fees.
4. Equity tranche principal.
5. Balance to the Chargor.

The equity tranche is **first loss**: it absorbs the shortfall before any senior loss arises.
