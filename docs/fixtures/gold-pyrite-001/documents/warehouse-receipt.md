## Lender's note

This receipt is **non-negotiable**. It evidences custody and attornment, not title, and is
not itself a document of title. Security in the goods on land rests on the attornment plus
the security agreement at document D18; security in the goods afloat rests on possession of
the bills of lading at document D08. Peru has no functioning negotiable warehouse-receipt
statute of the UNIDROIT 2024 Model Law type, which is why the structure relies on attornment
and possession rather than on a negotiable receipt.
