**Date** 12 August 2026

## Part 1 — Deed of Assignment

### Parties

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| **Assignor** | Open Mineral AG, as seller of record under the offtake, and Minera Cerro Pyrita S.A.C. as beneficial owner |
| **Assignee** | Pipeline Collateral Trust, BVI, as Security Trustee |

### 1. Assignment

The Assignor assigns to the Assignee **by way of security** all present and future rights,
title and interest in and to the **Assigned Receivables**, meaning every amount payable by
Beihai Jinyuan Smelting Co., Ltd under offtake contract OM-BJY-2026-0417, including:

1. each **provisional payment** under clause 10.4 of the offtake;
2. the **QP-final settlement** under clause 10.5, whether positive or negative;
3. any **price adjustment, umpire adjustment, weight adjustment or penalty rebate**;
4. any **damages, interest or indemnity** payable for breach of the offtake;
5. any **proceeds of the letter of credit** LC/BOC-GX/26/004417; and
6. any **insurance proceeds** under document D12 to the extent they relate to the Goods.

### 2. Legal, not equitable

This is intended to take effect as a **legal assignment under section 136 of the Law of
Property Act 1925**. Written notice has been given to the Buyer and acknowledged (Part 2).

### 3. Controlled account

All Assigned Receivables are payable exclusively to:

> **Pipeline Collateral Account — PIPE-GPC-001**
> Credit Agricole CIB, Geneva · SWIFT CRLYCHGGXXX · IBAN CH00 0000 0000 0000 0000 0

The Account Bank has agreed in the Account Bank Agreement of even date that the Assignee has
**sole signing authority** over the account and that the Account Bank waives all rights of
set-off, combination and lien over the balance.

### 4. Application

Amounts credited to the controlled account are applied in the order at clause 7 of the
facility agreement. The Assignee releases the balance to the Assignor after the Secured
Obligations due at that date are met.

### 5. Reassignment

On irrevocable discharge of the Secured Obligations the Assignee reassigns the Assigned
Receivables to the Assignor at the Assignor's cost.

### 6. Assignor's undertakings

Not to vary, waive, terminate or assign the offtake or any Assigned Receivable without the
Assignee's consent. To pay over immediately, on trust for the Assignee, anything received
into any other account.

## Lender's note

This is the single most load-bearing document in the security package. Physical collateral in
a foreign jurisdiction is slow and expensive to enforce; the assigned receivable is cash into
a controlled account the Security Trustee already signs on. The countersigned acknowledgement
at Part 3 converts the assignment from equitable to legal and closes the set-off defence,
which is the usual point of failure in commodity trade finance.
