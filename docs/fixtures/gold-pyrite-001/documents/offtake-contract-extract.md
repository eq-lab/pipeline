## 3. Material

Refractory gold-pyrite flotation concentrate of Peruvian origin, produced at the Cerro
Pyrita plant, of the typical specification at Annex A.

## 4. Quantity

5,200 dry metric tonnes, plus or minus 5% in Seller's option, in two lots of approximately
2,600 dmt. Lots are drawn from a single homogenised bonded stockpile at Callao and are
treated as one settlement parcel for assay purposes (clause 11.6).

## 5. Delivery

**CFR Fangchenggang, Guangxi, PRC (Incoterms 2020.)** Seller pays freight to the named port.
Risk passes on loading at Callao.

**5.4 Title.** Title to each lot passes to Buyer on the earlier of (a) Buyer's payment of the
provisional invoice in full, and (b) Buyer's endorsement of the full set of original bills of
lading. The Incoterm governs delivery, risk and cost allocation only and does not transfer
title.

## 6. Payable metals

| Metal | Payable | Minimum deduction |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | **78.0%** of the settlement Au content | **1.00 g per dmt** |
| Silver | **85.0%** of the settlement Ag content | **30.00 g per dmt** |

Payable metal per dry metric tonne is `(grade × payable% − minimum deduction)`, floored at
zero. No other metal is payable. Iron and sulphur carry no credit under this contract
notwithstanding their value to Buyer's acid plant.

## 7. Treatment charge

**USD 210.00 per dry metric tonne** of concentrate delivered. Fixed for the term. No price
participation, no escalator.

## 8. Refining charges

| Metal | Refining charge |
|---|---|
| Gold | **USD 6.00 per payable troy ounce** |
| Silver | **USD 0.40 per payable troy ounce** |

## 9. Penalties

Penalties are assessed **flat and pro rata**, in USD per dry metric tonne, on the amount by
which the settlement assay exceeds the threshold. No penalty applies at or below threshold.

| Element | Threshold | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Arsenic (As) | above 0.20% | USD 5.00 per dmt for each 0.10% |
| Lead (Pb) | above 1.00% | USD 1.00 per dmt for each 0.10% |
| Zinc (Zn) | above 2.00% | USD 1.00 per dmt for each 0.50% |
| Antimony (Sb) | above 0.10% | USD 1.00 per dmt for each 0.01% |
| Mercury (Hg) | above 10 ppm | USD 1.00 per dmt for each 5 ppm |
| Bismuth (Bi) | above 300 ppm | USD 1.00 per dmt for each 10 ppm |
| Cadmium (Cd) | above 50 ppm | USD 1.00 per dmt for each 10 ppm |

**9.4 Rejection.** Buyer may reject a lot assaying above 3.50% As, above 0.50% Sb, above
100 ppm Hg, or below 15.0 g/dmt Au.

## 10. Pricing

**10.1 Reference.** Gold: the **LBMA Gold Price PM (USD)**. Silver: the **LBMA Silver Price
(USD)**.

**10.2 Quotational period.** The **second calendar month following the month of arrival**
of the vessel at the discharge port ("2 MAMA"), being the arithmetic average of all published
fixings in that month.

**10.3 Provisional pricing.** The provisional invoice prices at the average of the fixings
over the five business days preceding the bill of lading date.

**10.4 Provisional payment.** **90%** of the provisional invoice value, payable at sight
against the documents at clause 13, under the letter of credit at clause 12.

**10.5 Final settlement.** Within 10 business days after the last day of the quotational
period, on the settlement assay and settlement weight, less amounts already paid. A negative
balance is refunded by Seller within the same period.

## 11. Weighing, sampling and assaying

**11.1 Weight** is determined by draft survey at the load port by the independent
superintendent, in wet metric tonnes, and converted to dry metric tonnes as
`dmt = wmt × (1 − moisture fraction)`. Load-port weight is final.

**11.2 Sampling** follows ISO 12743. Moisture follows ISO 10251. Given the coarse gold
fraction, the sampling protocol includes screen assay for material retained on 106 µm.

**11.3 Sample splitting.** Four parts: Buyer, Seller, umpire, and a reserve retained by the
superintendent for not less than **three months**.

**11.4 Assay exchange** within **45 days** of the discharge date, exchanged simultaneously.

**11.5 Splitting limits.** Gold **0.50 g/dmt**. Silver **3.0 g/dmt**. Arsenic **0.10%**.
Moisture **0.30 percentage points**. Within the limit, the settlement figure is the
arithmetic mean of the two results. Outside the limit, the umpire sample is assayed by an
umpire from the panel at clause 11.7 and the settlement figure is the mean of the umpire
result and whichever party result is closer to it. The umpire result is final and binding.

**11.6 Composite settlement.** Both lots derive from one homogenised stockpile. The parties
adopt a single composite settlement assay for the whole parcel, applied to each lot.

**11.7 Umpire panel.** Alex Stewart International, Intertek LSI, Bureau Veritas Inspectorate,
in rotation. **The firm that carried out the sampling may not act as umpire.** Umpire fees
are borne by the party whose result is further from the umpire result.

## 12. Payment security

Buyer shall establish, not later than 15 days before the first laycan, an irrevocable
documentary letter of credit at sight in Seller's favour, in a form acceptable to Seller,
issued by a bank acceptable to Seller and confirmed by a bank of Seller's choosing, covering
the provisional payments for both lots.

## 13. Documents for payment

Full set 3/3 original bills of lading, made out to order and blank endorsed; provisional
commercial invoice; packing list and lot manifest; certificate of weight and moisture;
certificate of analysis; certificate of origin; insurance certificate for 110% of invoice
value; IMSBC declaration and TML certificate.

## 14. Assignment

Seller may assign its rights to payment under this contract. Buyer shall, on receipt of a
notice of assignment substantially in the form at Annex D, pay the assigned amounts to the
account nominated in the notice and shall not seek to set off any claim arising after the
date of the notice. Buyer acknowledges the assignment recorded at document D17.

## 15. Term

One shipment parcel of 5,200 dmt, both lots to be shipped by 30 November 2026. This contract
is a call-off under frame agreement OM-BJY-2025-FR-08 dated 3 March 2025, under which Buyer
has contracted for not less than 24,000 dmt per calendar year.

## 16. Force majeure, sanctions and compliance

Standard LME-style force majeure. Both parties warrant they are not designated under OFAC,
EU, UN or UK sanctions and will not perform in a manner that would expose the other to
designation.
