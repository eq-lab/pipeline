## 2. Type of arrangement

This is a **full Collateral Management Agreement (CMA)**, not a Stock Monitoring Agreement.
The Collateral Manager takes and retains **exclusive physical possession and control** of the
Goods. The Depositor has no access except under escort and no power of release.

Opening balance on 12 August 2026: **5,652.174 wmt / 5,200.000 dmt**.

## 5. Collateral Manager's undertakings

5.1 To hold the Goods **to the order of the Security Trustee alone**.

5.2 To release Goods **only** against a **Release Instruction** signed by the Security
Trustee in the form at Schedule 2. An instruction from the Depositor, the Facility Agent or
any other person has no effect.

5.3 To keep the Goods physically segregated and to permit no commingling with any other
party's stock.

5.4 To issue a **Warehouse Receipt** in the form at Schedule 1 on each deposit, and to issue
**no more than one** receipt in respect of any quantity of Goods. To confirm in writing, on
request, that no other receipt, trust receipt, delivery order or attornment has been issued
against the Goods. **This is the anti-double-pledge control.**

5.5 To deliver a **weekly stock report** to the Security Trustee stating quantity in wmt and
dmt, moisture, movements in and out, and seal integrity. To report **within 4 hours** any
discrepancy, seal breach, unauthorised access, third-party claim, attachment, or notice from
any authority.

5.6 To maintain professional indemnity cover of not less than **USD 20,000,000** per event,
naming the Security Trustee as an additional insured, and to deliver evidence annually.

5.7 To permit the Security Trustee and its inspectors access at any time without notice.

## 6. Depositor's undertakings

The Depositor warrants that it has full title to the Goods, free of encumbrance other than
the security created under document D18, and that no part of the Goods has been sold,
pledged, or made the subject of any other warehouse receipt or delivery order.

## 8. Fees

USD 4,800 per month plus USD 0.45 per wmt handled, payable by the Depositor. Non-payment does
not create a lien over the Goods in the Collateral Manager's favour. **The Collateral Manager
waives every lien and right of set-off over the Goods.**

## 9. Term and termination

From 10 August 2026 until the Security Trustee confirms in writing that the Secured
Obligations are discharged. The Collateral Manager may terminate on 60 days' notice to the
Security Trustee, and must continue to hold the Goods until a replacement collateral manager
has been appointed and has attorned to the Security Trustee.
