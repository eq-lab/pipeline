## Lender's note

The credit is the payment protection, not the collateral. Security in the goods comes from
the bills of lading and the CMA (documents D08, D15, D16, D18); security in the money comes
from the assignment (document D17). The issuing bank's undertaking runs to the beneficiary,
so the assignment of proceeds and the account instruction at 47A(3) are what route the cash
into the controlled account.
