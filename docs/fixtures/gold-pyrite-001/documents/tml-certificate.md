## Declaration

The moisture content of each lot at the time of loading is below the Transportable Moisture
Limit. The cargo is therefore acceptable for carriage under the IMSBC Code without the
special precautions required for cargo at or above the TML.

Sampling for the can test at the point of loading is to be carried out by the Master or the
Master's representative in accordance with IMSBC section 8.

## Notes for the lender

1. A Group A cargo at or above its TML **may not legally sail**. Moisture is therefore a
   binary gate on the cargo's realisable value, not merely a weight adjustment.
2. The certificate is valid for six months from issue and must be renewed if the cargo is
   restowed, rained on in an open stockpile, or held past 12 March 2027.
3. Moisture is separately load-bearing on valuation through the wet-to-dry conversion at
   document D05.
